Case study · Manufacturing websites

Trailer brands

A family of brand websites for North American trailer manufacturers, each with a product catalogue, dealer finder and quote requests.

IndustryTrailer manufacturing
BrandsCam Superline, Formula, Look and more
ServicesBrand sites, catalogue, dealer finder
Livecamsuperline.com
1. Cam Superline2. Formula3. Look4. Cargo Express5. Impact
The brief

Many brands, one system.

Each trailer brand needed its own look, but dealers and buyers expect the same things on every site: models, specs, a dealer near them and a fast way to ask for a quote.

We built a shared structure and gave every brand its own colours, photography and hero, so new brands launch quickly and stay consistent.

What we built

Every step, designed.

  • 02Trailer catalogue by category with specs
  • 03Dealer finder
  • 04Request-a-quote flow on every page
  • 05Careers and resources sections
  • 06Fast, mobile-first pages
