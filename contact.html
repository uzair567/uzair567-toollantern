Questions
How fast can you start?

Usually the same week. Small websites go live in one to two weeks once we have your content.

Which platform should I choose?

WordPress if you want to edit pages yourself, Shopify for a simple store, and custom Next.js when speed and flexibility matter most.

Do I need to write the content?

No. Send rough notes or your old site and we write and arrange the copy. You approve every page.

How does payment work?

Small projects: a deposit and a final payment at launch. Larger systems: milestone payments as each stage is approved.

What happens after launch?

30 days of free fixes, then optional monthly care for updates and backups.
