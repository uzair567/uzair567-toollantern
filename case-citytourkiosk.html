City Tour Kiosk

A touchscreen booking kiosk for hotels and malls, and the website behind it. Tourists choose a country, a city and an experience, then book and pay.

ClientCity Tour Kiosk
MarketsNew York · Benidorm
ServicesKiosk UI, store, dashboards
Livecitytourkiosk.com
1. Welcome2. Country3. City4. Experiences5. Details
The brief

65+ experiences, sold from a screen.

City Tour needed one platform that works on self-service kiosks in hotels and on the web, with many independent vendors behind it.

We designed a bold, touch-first interface that tourists understand in seconds, and connected it to a multi-vendor booking engine with payments and confirmations.

What we built

Every step, designed.

  • 01Touch-first kiosk flow: country, city, experience, booking
  • 02Filters by air, water and land with live prices and savings
  • 03Experience pages with location, duration and what is included
  • 04Card payments, deposits and 24-hour advance rules per vendor
  • 05Confirmation emails to customer, vendor and admin
  • 06Admin, vendor and location dashboards, plus a partner portal
