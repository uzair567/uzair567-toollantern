Recruitment agencies

Websites for UK recruitment agencies in logistics, healthcare and education, built to win both employers and candidates.

The brief

Two audiences, one clear site.

Recruitment sites have to speak to employers who need staff and candidates who need work, often on the same page.

We give each audience its own path, with sector pages, simple forms and a strong call to action above the fold.

What we built

Every step, designed.

  • 01Separate journeys for employers and candidates
  • 02Sector pages for logistics, care, education and more
  • 03CV upload and staff request forms
  • 04Bold, sector-specific branding
  • 05Mobile-first layouts for candidates on the go
