Business systems

Five working systems for pharmacies, clinics, retail and construction: point of sale, stock, billing, staff roles and cash-flow reporting in one place.

The brief

Software built around how the business works.

Small businesses often run on spreadsheets and paper. Each of these systems replaces that with one screen the owner and staff use every day.

We design the main dashboard first, agree it with the client, then build each module with logins and roles for the team.

What we built

Every step, designed.

  • 01Point of sale with receipts and cash-drawer control
  • 02Batch-level stock and expiry tracking with low-stock alerts
  • 03Patients, appointments, billing and staff roles for clinics
  • 04Supplier purchases, payables and customer credit
  • 05Cash position, receivables and project fund tracking
  • 06Reports, exports and printable invoices
